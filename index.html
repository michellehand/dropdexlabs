Welcome to the Archive.

DropDex is an ongoing investigation into the anatomy of the drop. Every entry begins as a moment in a track — a release, a rupture, a crowd response — then becomes data: measured, classified, ranked, and archived.

The question is simple. Why do certain drops hit harder than others? The answer is still being documented.

Archive Protocol

Document. Analyze. Classify.

DropDex studies bass drops as measurable events. Each drop is isolated, examined, and scored across traits like weight, shock, aggression, brightness, and tension. The result is a growing archive of ranked drops, audio evidence, classifications, and collectible battle cards.

Document

Log each drop as an archive entry with track details, timing, class, rank, and observed behavior.

Analyze

Measure audio features that describe impact, build-up, frequency energy, and release.

Classify

Translate the data into rankings, drop classes, rarity tiers, and collectible card profiles.

Collect

Turn the ranked results into battle cards with stats, rarity, artwork, and archive links.

Model Basics

The model does not chase hype. It records behavior.

DropDex does not decide whether a song is good. It measures how a drop behaves: how heavy it lands, how much tension it releases, and how intense the audio signature becomes. The model turns those observations into a repeatable ranking system.

Weight

Low-end mass, bass pressure, and perceived heaviness.

Shock

The immediate release of energy when the drop hits.

Aggression

Intensity, attack, roughness, and force in the drop section.

Tension

How strongly the build-up prepares the listener for impact.

Brightness

High-frequency energy, presence, and sharpness.

Power

The combined DropDex score used for global ranking.
