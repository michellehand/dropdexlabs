DropDex

The Statistical Archive of Bass Drops.

DropDex analyzes bass drops as measurable audio events. A drop is isolated, visualized as a spectrogram, measured across audio traits, scored through a repeatable model, and archived as a ranked record.

The question is simple: why do certain drops hit harder than others? The answer is still being documented.
