DropDex Classification System

Scores, Classes & Signature Traits

Every archived drop is translated into five core scores, an acoustic fingerprint, and a primary and secondary class. Together, they describe how the specimen builds, strikes, moves, and holds pressure.

Scores summarize the major dimensions. Classes identify the dominant acoustic archetype. Traits reveal the measurable behaviors underneath both.
Arena Rules

01 // Core Score System

Card Scores

Each score condenses several measured acoustic behaviors into a readable card-level dimension. No single score fully defines a drop.

PWR

Power

The drop's raw physical force. Power describes how hard the specimen arrives through its low-end weight, impact shock, brightness, and aggression.

Weight · Shock · Brightness · Aggression
BLD

Build

The strength of the approach into the drop. Build measures how effectively tension and charge-up create anticipation before impact.

Tension · Charge-Up
PRD

Production

The execution and sound-design behavior of the drop, including its spatial scale, sharpness, complexity, continuity, and control.

Width · Attack · Density · Texture · Sustain · Flow
TON

Tonality

The strength and movement of pitched musical information, from melodic presence and harmonic richness to tonal motion and payoff.

Melodic Presence · Harmonic Richness · Tonal Motion · Melodic Payoff
STY

Style

The strength of the specimen's expressive identity. Style reflects how clearly the drop develops recognizable behavioral patterns such as groove, filth, fluidity, technical complexity, cinematic scale, instability, and mass.

Groove · Nasty · Melt · Technical · Cinematic · Wonk · Mass

02 // Acoustic Archetypes

Drop Classes

Each specimen receives a primary and secondary class based on the strongest archetypal identities detected in the analysis.

01

Impact Cannon

A direct, force-heavy drop defined by sudden sub impact, physical weight, and aggressive arrival.

Shock · Weight · Aggression
02

Pressure Engine

A sustained low-end system that accumulates pressure and maintains mass after the drop lands.

Pressure Tension · Mass · Sustain
03

Vacuum Striker

A tension-driven specimen that withdraws energy before releasing a sudden, high-contrast strike.

Vacuum Tension · Charge-Up · Shock
04

Filth Monster

A distorted and hostile drop built around abrasive texture, aggression, and deliberately nasty sound design.

Nasty · Texture · Aggression
05

Technical Weapon

A precise, information-dense specimen marked by sharp attacks, complex movement, and controlled detail.

Technical · Attack · Density
06

Groove Driver

A rhythm-led drop whose movement, pacing, and sustained pulse create a strong sense of forward motion.

Groove · Flow · Sustain
07

Cinematic

A wide, dramatic specimen that uses scale, tension, atmosphere, and sustained energy to create a larger-than-life payoff.

Cinematic · Melt · Width
08

Melodic Weapon

A rare tonal archetype where melody and harmony remain dominant against the drop's strongest physical characteristics.

Tonality · Melodic Presence · Harmonic Richness

03 // Acoustic Fingerprint

Signature Traits

Traits are normalized measurements derived from the build, pre-drop, impact, and post-drop windows of each archived specimen.

FORCE Impact & Physicality

Weight

The physical heaviness and low-end body carried by the drop.

Shock

The sudden jump in sub energy and loudness when the drop lands.

Brightness

The amount of high-frequency energy introduced at impact.

Aggression

The intensity, force, and hostility of the post-drop signal.

Mass

The combined sense of weight, width, low-end body, texture, and sustain.

Low-End Sustain

How strongly the drop preserves its bass energy after impact.

BUILD Tension & Release

Tension

The strongest detected form of anticipation before impact.

Vacuum Tension

Tension created by removing energy and opening space before the drop.

Pressure Tension

Tension created by accumulating loudness and pressure before release.

Charge-Up

The combined escalation of loudness, brightness, rhythm, risers, and silence.

CONSTRUCTION Production Behavior

Width

The perceived stereo scale and spatial reach of the drop.

Attack

The sharpness and immediacy of individual transient strikes.

Density

How much rhythmic and spectral information occupies the drop.

Texture

The complexity and variation present in the sound surface.

Sustain

How consistently the drop maintains energy after its initial strike.

Flow

The continuity and smoothness of movement across the drop window.

STYLE Behavioral Identity

Groove

Rhythmic movement created by balanced density, flow, sustain, and attack.

Nasty

Abrasive character created by texture, aggression, attack, and brightness.

Melt

A broad, sustained, fluid sound that spreads instead of striking cleanly.

Technical

Precision and complexity expressed through density, attack, motion, and detail.

Cinematic

Scale and drama created through width, tension, build, and sustained brightness.

Wonk

Unstable, irregular movement shaped by texture, density, attack, and flow.

TONALITY Melody & Harmony

Melodic Presence

How clearly pitched and harmonic content appears within the drop.

Harmonic Richness

The variety and complexity of the specimen's tonal structure.

Tonal Motion

How actively pitch and harmonic relationships change through time.

Melodic Payoff

The increase in tonal activity created when the build resolves into the drop.

Classification Note

No single metric defines a specimen.

Classes emerge from combinations of traits. Two drops may share the same class while reaching it through different acoustic fingerprints, creating distinct strengths, weaknesses, and Arena behavior.
