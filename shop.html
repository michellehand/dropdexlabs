FAQ

Before You Collect

What is a DropDex card?

A DropDex card is a collectible specimen based on a measured bass drop from the DropDex archive.

Are these official artist products?

No. DropDex is an independent archive and collector project.

Will rankings change?

Yes. Rankings reflect the current archive model and may evolve as more drops are analyzed.

When will cards be available?

The first physical card run is being prepared now. Follow DropDex for release updates.
