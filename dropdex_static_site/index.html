What DropDex Does

The archive for measured drops.

DropDex is a music analytics project that studies drop sections as data. Each drop is broken into measurable traits like weight, shock, aggression, brightness, and tension. Those metrics feed a ranking system and a collectible card format.

Analyze

Extract audio features from bass drops and compare them across tracks.

Rank

Score each drop with a custom model built around impact, build-up, and sonic force.

Collect

Turn the ranked results into battle cards with stats, rarity, artwork, and archive links.

Model Basics

No hype. Just data.

The model is designed to compare drops using consistent measurements instead of opinion. It does not decide whether a song is “good.” It measures how a drop behaves: how heavy it lands, how much tension it releases, and how intense the audio signature becomes.

Weight

Low-end mass, bass pressure, and perceived heaviness.

Shock

The immediate release of energy when the drop hits.

Aggression

Intensity, attack, roughness, and force in the drop section.

Tension

How strongly the build-up prepares the listener for impact.

Brightness

High-frequency energy, presence, and sharpness.

Power

The combined DropDex score used for global ranking.
